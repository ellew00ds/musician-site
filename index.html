Sterling Steffen, known professionally as DJ Sterling, is an Austin-based DJ, multi-instrumentalist, producer, and educator available for weddings and private events across Austin, TX and San Antonio. Growing up in Austin, he developed an eclectic ear spanning country, hip-hop, classical, and jazz. As a saxophonist he has performed with Sir Woman, Wild Child, Mother Falcon, Dr. Joe, and The Watters. His deep musicality and intentional set curation make every event feel alive — from the first dance to the last song of the night. Inquire for Austin wedding DJ availability and booking.
