About DJ Sterling

Sterling Steffen is an Austin-based DJ, multi-instrumentalist, and producer whose deep musicality sets him apart from typical wedding DJs. Growing up in Austin, he developed an eclectic ear spanning country, hip-hop, classical, and jazz, and as a working saxophonist who has performed with Sir Woman, Wild Child, and Mother Falcon, he understands exactly how music moves a room.

Every set is curated, not templated. Sterling works with each couple personally to understand their taste, their crowd, and the energy they want, then delivers a night that feels exactly right from the first song to the last.
